### History screen

The browsing history has a screen of its own, with the same shape as the downloads
screen: a title bar holding the title, a trash can that clears everything and a plain X
that closes, with every destructive action behind a confirmation dialog.

The rows lose the time column and the oversized type, and gain a trash can of their own
that deletes the entry. The new controls are wired into the D-pad focus graph instead of
being pointer-only: the header is walkable in both directions, and the list lets RIGHT
step onto a row's delete icon while LEFT/UP/DOWN hand the focus back.
