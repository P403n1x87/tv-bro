6Where everything landed installed

  • App icon — the galley alone on amber, rendered per density: res/drawable-{mdpi,hdpi,xhdpi,xxhdpi,xxxhdpi}/ic_launcher.png (48/72/96/144/192 px) and metadata/en-US/images/icon.png (192 px).
  • TV launcher banner — res/drawable-xhdpi/banner.png (640×360).
  • Store listing — metadata/en-US/images/featureGraphic.png (amber, 1024×500) and metadata/en-US/images/tvBanner.png (ink, 1280×720).
  • Home page — assets/pages/home/logo.png for the light theme and logo-dark.png for the dark theme, switched by a prefers-color-scheme rule in the page CSS.
  • README — docs/branding/readme-banner.png and readme-banner-dark.png (both 1200×300), chosen by a <picture> element at the top of README.md.
  • Lockups — not consumed by the app; kept as brand sources in artwork/variants/ (lockup-horizontal, lockup-stacked and their light recolours).

Regenerate everything with python3 artwork/build.py and check the pixels with python3 artwork/verify.py — it re-derives every variant from the source artwork and diffs all 13 installed files against their variant. artwork/source/odysseus-art.svg is a read-only copy of your file and has not been modified.
